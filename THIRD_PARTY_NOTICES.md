# Third-party scope

FEATM's MIT notice covers the project's own distributed code. Optional packages
such as NumPy, pandas, librosa, CuPy, Dask, PyTorch and Open3D retain their own
licenses. Installing a dependency is different from bundling its source into
FEATM. No dependency/model weights or datasets are included by these examples.

Dataset and model downloaders must retain the provider's terms, citations and
attribution. ESC-10's terms differ from the full ESC-50 dataset; see the linked
provider material in [practical examples](docs/PRACTICAL_EXAMPLES.md).

The supplied source has no vendored third-party package tree or explicit
third-party copyright header detected by this preparation review. That limited
inspection is not a complete code-origin or rights audit. Contributors should
identify any copied/adapted implementation and required notice for owner review
before it is redistributed; FEATM's MIT declaration does not override it.
